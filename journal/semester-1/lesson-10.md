## Reflection
1. **Why are logs, metrics, and traces all necessary?**  
They each provide differnt types of data that will help in locating where issues are occring in the code/application/stack  
1. **Why is structured logging better than plain text?**  
Querying structured objects is faster and less resource intensive then searching strings  
1. **Why are correlation IDs important?**  
The aid the investigator to quickly follow a single event stream  
1. **Why should secrets never appear in logs?**  
Anything gets access to the logs will have access to the secrets written to them
